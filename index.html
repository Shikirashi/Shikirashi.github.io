Projects

Astraventus: Kartikaris

A Unity-based information warfare simulator prototype exploring radar systems and artillery combat. Set in a procedurally populated forest, it features a walking mech with an artillery cannon that can fold for travel or deploy for aiming during gameplay.

Astraventus: Blades in Bloom

A Godot RPG prototype featuring cell-based overworld exploration and a combat system inspired by historical swordsmanship. An exploration into story-driven game development, combining RPG systems with more deliberate, stance-based swordplay.

Dungeon Streamer

A dungeon-crawling adventure where you play as an adventurer livestreaming your expeditions. Fight monsters through turn-based combat while managing your audience, with viewer statistics and a live chat accompanying your adventures.

Astraventus: Machine Maidens

An aerial combat prototype featuring ritual machines and witches riding flying brooms. Built around high-speed dogfights, with both PvP and PvE combat in mind.

Spears and Shields and Hordes of Enemies

A single-player combat prototype exploring physics-based spear and shield combat against hordes of enemies. Focused on positioning, defensive formations, and the physical interactions between weapons, shields, and enemies.

Ball Battalion

A lane-defense game where matching colored balls commands defenders of the same color to attack enemies advancing along their lanes. Combining ball-matching puzzles with defensive strategy.
