Projects

Selected games and prototypes. Screenshots show the actual projects at their current stages of development.

Kartikaris

A Unity mech prototype with a procedurally populated forest and a walking artillery machine. The cannon can fold for travel or deploy and aim during gameplay.

Blades in Bloom

A Godot RPG prototype with overworld exploration, NPC interactions and a separate real-time sword combat system.

Dungeon Streamer

A dungeon crawler played as a livestream, with turn-based encounters, viewer statistics and live audience chat. The v21.2 HTML version is playable; a Godot migration is in progress.

Witch Broom Dogfighting

An aerial combat prototype featuring broom flight, opposing riders, spellcasting and shields.

Ball Battalion

A lane-defense arcade puzzle where matching colored conveyor balls powers defenders. Playable on Android and Web, with matching conveyor balls powering defenders across four lanes.
